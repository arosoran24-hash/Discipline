Before you go

Read this once. It takes two minutes and it is what makes your visit useful.

  1. You are a normal guest. Never say you are assessing anyone. If asked, you are trying the spa for a day.
  2. Call first (stop 1), then visit. Use every facility for at least 10 minutes: gym, pool, sauna, steam room, jacuzzi, ice pool. Order something at the Pool Snack bar.
  3. Note the times. Fill this in while you sit — on a lounger or in the changing room, it looks like texting. Your answers are saved on this phone even if you close the browser.
  4. Photos only of signs, equipment or dirt when nobody is around. Never in changing rooms, never of other guests or staff faces.
  5. Keep every receipt. Day pass and anything you order.
  6. Scoring: Yes = fully met the standard. No = not met, even slightly. N/A = you could not check. Whenever you tap No, add a short note saying exactly what you saw and where.
  7. When you are done tap Finish, then Send — it opens WhatsApp with your report. Send it the same day.
Six tests to do during the visit
  1. On the phone, ask the opening hours, the women-only hours and the day-pass price.
  2. On arrival, ask for a tour or to be shown around.
  3. Ask any staff member how long you should stay in the ice pool and how to combine it with the sauna.
  4. Ask an attendant for an extra towel — time how long it takes.
  5. Make one small, polite complaint (for example ‘the jacuzzi feels a bit cool’ or ‘the sauna is not very hot’) and watch what happens.
  6. On your way out, notice whether anyone asks how your visit was.

Items marked Critical are safety or hygiene standards — be strict on those.
